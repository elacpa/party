Detective Tools

Each character is hiding something — but for completely different reasons. The truth only comes out when you connect what they're all avoiding. Use these to dig.

  • "You said ____, but yesterday you said ____. Help me connect those dots."
  • "You laughed right there — what was that laugh about?"
  • "You won't give a specific answer. Why are you keeping it general?"
  • "You keep zooming out. What are you avoiding talking about?"
  • "You paused before you said that. What were you about to say?"
  • "What do all five of us have a reason to hide right now?"

Evidence Notebook

Take notes as the others perform. Watch their body language. Listen for what they won't say. Each person is hiding a different piece of the same puzzle — and one of them might not even know they're hiding it.

Final Verdict

It's not about catching one liar. Each person is hiding something — for a different reason. What's the ONE truth that would explain why every single person, in every different way, is acting strange? → Hint: someone in this room may not even realize they're hiding it.
